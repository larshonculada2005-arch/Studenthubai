Features

Everything a student needs

Built specifically for students who want to stay on top of their academic life without the stress.

📋

Task Management

Add, edit, and track assignments with title, subject, description, deadline, and status. Swipe to delete.

📅

Calendar View

Interactive month calendar that highlights days with deadlines. Tap any day to see tasks due on that date.

🤖

AI Planner

Smart suggestions based on your real tasks — identifies urgent deadlines, in-progress work, and study focus areas.

📊

Analytics

Visual breakdown of your task completion rate, status distribution, and subject workload at a glance.

🛠️

Study Tools

Built-in Pomodoro timer with session tracking and a Flashcard builder to create and study Q&A cards.

🔔

Push Notifications

Deadline reminders via WorkManager — fires 24 hours before due dates even when the app is closed.

🔐

Secure Auth

Firebase Authentication with email/password and Google Sign-In. Password reset via email.

⚙️

Settings

Change password, update email, edit profile (name & course), theme, language, date format preferences.
